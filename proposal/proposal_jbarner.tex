\section{Problem and Decision Boundary}

A wheeled delivery robot plans over a campus sidewalk graph whose edge labels come from a
map prior (OpenStreetMap). Map labels can be stale or wrong; a shortest-path planner that trusts
an edge mislabeled \cl{footway} when it actually contains stairs or a curb commits the robot to an
untraversable, potentially damaging segment. \textbf{Decision:} for each edge $e$ the planner proposes, may the
robot commit to traversing $e$? The policy emits one of four outcomes:
\textbf{Act} (admit and traverse); \textbf{Defer} (unresolved obligation and closer observations
remain: continue approach and re-observe); \textbf{Repair} (an obligation fails, or remains
unresolved with no evidence left: reject $e$, remove it, replan, and record a proposed map patch);
\textbf{Handoff} (no admissible route to the goal remains: escalate to a remote operator).
Costs are asymmetric: admitting stairs is catastrophic, a false rejection costs a detour. The gate is
\emph{fail-closed}: an edge is inadmissible unless evidence affirmatively discharges every
obligation for its risk tier. \textbf{Research question:} can a calibrated, tiered multi-source
gate match a conservative ``reject if any source objects'' veto on false admission while reducing
unnecessary rejection and deferral, including under injected sensor and map faults?

\section{Modalities, Data, Typed Claims, Risks, and Obligations}

\textbf{Data.} \emph{GND}~\cite{gnd} (CC0). \textbf{Primary:} UMD map~3 (McKeldin Mall), north
session: a 24\,min, 1.9\,km Clearpath Jackal drive (bag chunks 04--14, $\sim$69\,GB) with a 32-beam
Ouster LiDAR (10\,Hz), wide-angle RGB (640$\times$480, 15\,Hz), GPS (5\,Hz; reported $\sigma$ median
9.6\,m), wheel/IMU odometry, 1,792 LiDAR-SLAM keyframe poses, and a human-labeled five-class
traversability map in which yellow marks \emph{stairs or curbs}~\cite{gnd} ($\sim$95 candidate stair
regions). \textbf{Held-out:} Georgetown (GTown; different campus and
sensors) as a shift condition.
\emph{OSM}~\cite{osm} (ODbL): footway graph and \cl{highway=steps} tags, cached once as GeoJSON.
\textbf{Head labels:} weak positives where an OSM stair lies 3--25\,m ahead, split by location with buffers;
the GND map stays out of training. GPS estimate, ignoring occlusion: $\sim$20 route sites, $\sim$840 positive frames.
Bags are converted once with the pure-Python \cl{rosbags} library~\cite{rosbags}; no ROS or network
is needed afterward. \textbf{Alignment and ground truth:} the traversability map was annotated on a
top-down projection of the LiDAR global map~\cite{gnd}, so map pixels are tied to the SLAM frame by a
2D similarity transform fitted once (ground-removed top view $\leftrightarrow$ obstacle layer); GPS is
kept out of the ground-truth path and serves only as independent evidence for C2. Observations are
associated with edges in this world frame (camera and LiDAR via GND's published extrinsics). An edge is a ground-truth hazard if its footprint intersects yellow:
thin yellow lines are labeled curb/step (T1) and yellow regions $\ge A_{\min}$ are labeled stairs (T2).
This curb/stairs split is my own definition, not GND's.

\textbf{Typed claims.} Every claim is a typed record with fields \cl{edge}, \cl{predicate},
\cl{value}, \cl{uncertainty}, \cl{source}, \cl{tier}, and \cl{provenance} (sensor, timestamp, frame
index, SHA-256 of the cached artifact, and model/checker version).

\textbf{Risk tiers.} An edge's tier is the \emph{maximum} hazard asserted by any source (escalation
only; no source can lower a tier): \textbf{T0} flat path; \textbf{T1} curb, single step, or curb cut;
\textbf{T2} stairs. GND notes that wheeled robots cannot traverse stairs or curbs~\cite{gnd}, so the
platform step limit $h_{\text{lim}}$ is set conservatively below curb height (0.05\,m, configurable).

\textbf{Verification obligations} (all must be \textsc{pass}; any \textsc{fail} $\Rightarrow$
Repair; any \textsc{unresolved} $\Rightarrow$ Defer, or Repair if no closer evidence remains):
\textbf{O1} (all tiers) attribution: C2 confirms the fused GPS+odometry $2\sigma$ ellipse intersects only
$e$, which matters because ramps often run parallel to stairs. \textbf{O2} (T1+) geometry: C1 reports
$h_{\max} < h_{\text{lim}}$ with point coverage $\ge n_{\min}$. \textbf{O3} (T2) corroboration: the
calibrated $p(\text{stairs}) < \tau_2$ \emph{and} O2 holds on $\ge 2$ frames at distinct ranges.
\textbf{O4} (all tiers) provenance: every claim used resolves to a hashed cached artifact.
\textbf{Risks:} noisy UMD GPS ($\sigma\approx$10\,m; C2 fuses odometry; residual deferral measured); few independent stair sites (counted from SLAM poses; Wilson CIs on T2; achieved calibration bound reported); map-to-SLAM alignment error (residual reported, boundary edges flagged);
no camera--LiDAR transforms in the bags (claims fused per edge in the world frame, never per pixel);
OSM label errors shared with the map prior (disjoint locations; OSM--GND agreement reported);
UMD$\to$GTown shift degrading the camera (caught by O2/O3, and measured).

\section{Checkers, Action Gate, Invariant, and Failure Injection}

\textbf{Two independent checkers} use disjoint evidence and code paths.
\textbf{C1, geometric step-height:} fits a local ground plane to LiDAR points on the edge footprint and
reports the maximum surface discontinuity; it involves no learning and ignores semantic labels.
\textbf{C2, localization attribution:} fuses GPS with wheel/IMU odometry in a Kalman filter and uses
the fused covariance and graph geometry to decide whether an observation belongs to a single edge. \textbf{Gate:} a pure, deterministic function
$(\text{claims}, \text{tier}) \mapsto$ \{Act, Defer, Repair, Handoff\}. Missing evidence yields
\textsc{unresolved}, never \textsc{pass}. Deferral is real in replay: it consumes the next cached
observation of $e$ at closer range. \textbf{Invariant I1:} \emph{no edge is admitted unless every
obligation for its final tier is \textup{\textsc{pass}}, the tier is at least the maximum asserted by any
claim, and every supporting claim's hash resolves.} I1 is checked on every receipt by
\cl{validate --strict} and by property-based tests over randomized claim sets.
\textbf{Repair:} the planner removes the rejected edge and replans; the receipt records a proposed map
patch (\cl{tag(e): footway$\to$steps}) with its supporting evidence.
\textbf{Receipt:} \cl{receipt.json} records config and data-manifest hashes, seed, injected faults,
and per-edge claims, obligation states, decision, A3 attribution, and I1 result.

\textbf{Failure injection} (each paired with its expected detection and declared in the receipt):
\emph{missing}: camera or LiDAR dropout ($\Rightarrow$ \textsc{unresolved}, never admit);
\emph{corrupted}: blur, low light, glare~\cite{imagenetc}; LiDAR thinning; GPS bias of 3--10\,m and
random-walk drift ($\Rightarrow$ O1 fails); \emph{stale map}: flip $k\%$ of curb and stair edges to
\cl{footway}; \emph{shifted}: unseen UMD locations, UMD$\to$GTown campus; \emph{adversarial evidence substitution}:
flip the map \emph{and} replace stair image crops with flat-sidewalk crops, so that two of three sources
agree on ``flat'' and only C1 stands between the robot and the stairs.
\textbf{Explicit unwarranted claims:} no claim of real-world deployment safety; of generalization
beyond the two evaluated campuses; of GTown stair rates ($\sim$2 regions); of detecting hazards other than steps, stairs, and curbs (ice, potholes,
pedestrians); of step heights below LiDAR resolution; or of physical causation. A3 attributions
describe the decision procedure, not the world.

\section{Metrics, Baselines, Ownership, Timeline, and Fallback}

\textbf{Task performance} (reported separately): stairs AUROC/F1 and ECE on held-out GND locations, and C1 agreement with
ground-truth step edges. \textbf{Policy behavior:} false admission rate (primary; Wilson 95\% CI),
unnecessary rejection, unnecessary deferral, handoff rate, checker coverage (fraction of admitted
edges whose obligations were all discharged by an executable checker), failure detection (fraction of
injected faults flagged in the receipt), repair success (replanned route reaches the goal over
ground-truth-traversable edges only; overhead relative to an oracle-map route), runtime, and peak RSS.
\textbf{Baselines:} B0 map-only shortest path; B1 camera-only threshold; B2 conservative veto (reject
if any source objects); \textbf{B3} the proposed calibrated tiered gate; plus an oracle-map route for
reference. \textbf{A1/A2/A3:} consume cached embeddings as representation evidence and extend them
with a UMD$\to$GTown embedding-shift score that is itself a typed claim (A1); consume calibrated
evidence and extend it to multi-source, per-tier thresholds that bound false admission on a
calibration split~\cite{conformal} (A2); consume the typed causal claim and extend it to per-decision
attribution (``$e$ rejected \emph{because of} C1''), verified exactly by deterministic counterfactual
re-runs of the gate with each source ablated (A3).
\textbf{Track ownership:} solo; I own all tracks.

\textbf{Timeline.} \emph{Oct 31--Nov 6:} lockfile, extraction of UMD north chunks 04--14, map alignment,
OSM cache, smoke config. \emph{Nov 7--13:} C1, C2, receipt schema, B0/B2. \emph{Nov 14--20:}
embeddings, head, calibration, tiered gate and policy (B1/B3). \emph{Nov 21--30:} injection harness,
I1 property tests, measured runtime and memory. \textbf{Dec 1 progress gate:} all gate items.
\emph{Dec 2--10:} full evaluation, A3 attribution, poster (Dec 11). \emph{Dec 12--15:} report.
\textbf{Fallbacks:} if map-to-SLAM alignment fails, align by hand-picked control points or
hand-label $\sim$200 edges from the GND map; if GND's extrinsics cannot be found, assume coincident
base and chassis frames and validate by LiDAR ground-plane height; if the camera head underperforms, report it and rely on
the minimum (no-model) fallback; if fused localization still leaves most edges unresolved, relax O1
to parallel-edge ambiguity; if UMD alignment fails, GTown becomes primary.

\section{Optional Scale-Up Justification (separately labeled; not on the graded path)}

No scale-up is required, and every graded result comes from the CPU baseline. If central resources
are awarded after the progress gate, I would request $\le$4 GPU hours to fine-tune the terrain head
end to end on the OSM-labeled training locations, to test whether task-specific features lower unnecessary rejection at equal false
admission. Outputs would be cached as frozen embeddings and replayed deterministically, and GPU usage
would be reported separately. No paid API is requested.
